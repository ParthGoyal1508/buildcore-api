## 1 — The coincidental quantity

The risk: all six measurement factors default to 1, so their product is 1, which is
indistinguishable from "one day served". A quantity that is right by coincidence passes every test
written the obvious way and becomes wrong the first time somebody sets a factor.

- [ ] CHK001 Is "MUST NOT read the six factors" expressed as an observable property of the output,
      or only as a prohibition on the implementation that a reviewer has to take on trust?
      [Measurability, Spec §FR-030b]
- [ ] CHK002 Does any requirement state what the quantity in force **must be** when a presence-paid
      line carries factor values other than 1 — reached by a seed script, a migration, a hand-edit
      or a future endpoint, rather than through the create API? The prohibition forbids reading
      them; nothing states the expected answer once they are non-default. [Gap, Spec §FR-030b]
- [ ] CHK003 **Do FR-003 and FR-030b contradict each other?** FR-003 requires the system to
      "compute **every** measurement line's quantity itself, as the product of the six factors";
      FR-030b forbids reading those factors for a presence-paid line. One of the two must be
      narrowed. [Conflict, Spec §FR-003, §FR-030b]
- [ ] CHK004 Is the two-shape input constraint — that a presence line **cannot carry** factors,
      rather than merely having them ignored — stated as a requirement, or does it exist only as a
      design decision in the plan and a refusal code in the contract?
      [Traceability, Spec §FR-030b, Contract `DWR_FACTORS_ON_PRESENCE_LINE`]
- [ ] CHK005 Is the database-level invariant (exactly one of the two quantities present, matching
      the payment basis) required anywhere in the **spec**, or only designed in data-model.md? A
      constraint that no requirement asks for is a constraint a later simplification removes.
      [Gap, Data-model §DWRTask]
- [ ] CHK006 Is the invariant's condition stated precisely enough to admit one implementation only
      — specifically, whether a `work_basis` line may carry a served quantity of null *and* an
      actual quantity of null (a line with nothing measured at all)?
      [Clarity, Spec §FR-030a, Data-model §DWRTask]
- [ ] CHK007 Is "a full day" given a value? FR-030c requires a remark when the served quantity is
      "less than a full day", and the contract shows `1` — but no requirement states that a full day
      is 1 rather than, say, the line's own daily rate basis. [Ambiguity, Spec §FR-030c]

## 2 — The omitted BOQ line

The risk: a line absent from a response and a line that measured nothing are indistinguishable to
the caller, and the caller is a bill.

- [ ] CHK008 Is "every BOQ line in the project" defined? The requirement does not say whether it
      means every line under every group, every line regardless of group, or every line meeting
      some condition. [Clarity, Spec §FR-037]
- [ ] CHK009 Are unpriced lines (rate still 0) explicitly included or excluded? 018's billing model
      already distinguishes `unpriced` as "nobody has priced this" rather than "this is free", and
      FR-037 is silent on whether such a line appears. [Gap, Spec §FR-037]
- [ ] CHK010 Is the requirement testable **without** already knowing the project's line count? An
      assertion over a returned list passes just as happily over a short list, which is the exact
      vacuity this feature's other guards were written to avoid. [Measurability, Spec §FR-037]
- [ ] CHK011 Is the non-vacuity measure — that the returned line count equals the project's own BOQ
      line count — stated as a requirement, or does it exist only as an instruction in
      quickstart.md Pass 6? [Traceability, Spec §FR-037, Quickstart Pass 6]
- [ ] CHK012 Are requirements defined for a project with **no** BOQ lines at all, and for a date
      range in which the project did not yet exist? [Coverage, Edge Case, Gap]
- [ ] CHK013 Is the behaviour specified for a measurement line that references no BOQ line (FR-007
      permits them) when the period figures are assembled — are such measurements reported
      anywhere, or silently invisible? FR-007 says they feed no period figure; nothing says whether
      a caller is told they exist. [Coverage, Spec §FR-007, §FR-034]

## 3 — The drifting counter

The risk: `doneQty` is denormalised, moved by approval and reversal, and therefore able to disagree
with the aggregate. A report nobody acts on is a report that stops being read.

- [ ] CHK014 Is "MUST be able to report any discrepancy" specified as a concrete obligation — an
      endpoint, a scheduled check, a log line, a test — or is the phrasing satisfied by any of them?
      "Be able to" is a capability, not a behaviour. [Clarity, Measurability, Spec §FR-039]
- [ ] CHK015 Is a tolerance defined? `Decimal(18,3)` increments are exact, so the intended tolerance
      is presumably zero — but the requirement does not say so, and a reader implementing it with a
      floating-point comparison would not be contradicting anything written.
      [Clarity, Gap, Spec §FR-039]
- [ ] CHK016 **Does anything specify what is to be done when a discrepancy is found?** No
      requirement states who is told, whether the figure is repairable, or whether the aggregate or
      the counter is authoritative. A reconciliation report with no defined response is a number
      that is looked at twice. [Gap, Spec §FR-039]
- [ ] CHK017 Is the relationship between the two figures stated — specifically, which of the stored
      counter and the computed aggregate is the system of record when they disagree?
      [Ambiguity, Spec §FR-039, Research §6]
- [ ] CHK018 Is FR-021's floor (no negative done quantity) connected to drift? A reversal that
      subtracts exactly what it added can only go negative if the counter has already drifted, so
      FR-021 is in practice a drift requirement — but it is written as though it were about
      arithmetic. [Consistency, Spec §FR-021, §FR-019]

## 4 — The half-applied approval

The risk: a transaction that partly succeeds leaves a counter wrong with nothing to notice.

- [ ] CHK019 Does any requirement state how a partial failure is made **observable**, as opposed to
      merely prevented? FR-013 requires all-or-none and that the report stays submitted; nothing
      requires the caller or an operator to learn that an attempt failed, or which line caused it.
      [Gap, Spec §FR-013, US2 AC5]
- [ ] CHK020 Is "the report stays submitted" stated as the sole permitted outcome of a failed
      approval, or as one acceptable outcome among others? [Clarity, Spec §FR-013]
- [ ] CHK021 Is the concurrency property — two reports measuring one BOQ line, approved at the same
      moment, **both** landing — stated as a requirement, or only as an entry under Edge Cases and
      a row in the plan's test strategy? FR-015 mandates the mechanism (relative increments) and
      not the outcome. [Traceability, Spec §FR-015, §Edge Cases]
- [ ] CHK022 Is the at-most-once property of FR-014 specified for the case of two **simultaneous**
      approvals of the same report, as distinct from two sequential ones? The edge case names it;
      the requirement says only "however many times approval is attempted".
      [Coverage, Spec §FR-014]
- [ ] CHK023 Are requirements defined for the interaction between a reversal and a concurrent
      approval of a different report touching the same line? [Coverage, Edge Case, Gap]

## 5 — The skipped probe that reads as a pass

The risk: an isolation test that quietly does nothing. Both tables have carried `tenant_isolation`
since August and neither policy has ever been in force in a test run, because the development and CI
role is a superuser and Postgres exempts superusers unconditionally.

- [ ] CHK024 **Does any requirement state that a skipped isolation test must report as skipped and
      never as passed?** FR-040 requires the coverage and says nothing about how an un-runnable
      probe presents. The existing suites warn and return, which a CI summary renders as a pass.
      [Gap, Spec §FR-040]
- [ ] CHK025 Is the non-vacuity obligation — that the probe role was actually created, asserted
      before anything else is asserted — a stated requirement, or a convention inherited by copying
      an existing file? Without it the suite passes against a superuser connection and proves
      nothing. [Traceability, Spec §FR-040]
- [ ] CHK026 Does FR-040 determine **which** tables must be covered? "Every table this feature
      writes" includes `BOQTaskItem`, whose `doneQty` this feature moves — so the requirement as
      written may oblige a probe for a table the feature otherwise does not own.
      [Clarity, Spec §FR-040]
- [ ] CHK027 Is the obligation stated for tables that already carry the policy but have never had it
      exercised, as distinct from newly created tables? This feature creates no table, so a reading
      of "every table this feature writes MUST carry the policy" is satisfied by doing nothing.
      [Ambiguity, Spec §FR-040]
- [ ] CHK028 Is the 404-not-403 rule of FR-029 testable as specified — is it stated that the
      distinction must hold for *every* endpoint, including the period figures and the
      reconciliation read? [Coverage, Spec §FR-029]

## 6 — FR-020's deferred half

The risk: a requirement that reads as implemented while its stated condition is undetectable.

- [ ] CHK029 **Do FR-020 and research §4 agree?** FR-020 requires refusal of "an approved report
      **whose measurement has been claimed on a bill**". Research §4 states that no column anywhere
      links a bill line to the measurement it consumed, and implements a billed-quantity floor
      instead. These are different conditions, and the spec carries only the first.
      [Conflict, Spec §FR-020, Research §4]
- [ ] CHK030 Would a reader of spec.md alone conclude that provenance was implemented? The
      qualification exists in research.md and in the contract's block quote, neither of which is
      the requirements document. [Consistency, Spec §FR-020]
- [ ] CHK031 Is what feature 023 must decide about this linkage recorded as an obligation on 023, or
      only as a remark inside a research rationale that 023 is not required to read?
      [Traceability, Gap, Research §4]
- [ ] CHK032 Is the floor's own scope specified — which bill statuses count as "left draft", and
      whether a subcontractor bill reaching the line through a work-order award line is included?
      The contract names them; no requirement does. [Clarity, Spec §FR-020, Contract §reverse]

## 7 — The report number

- [ ] CHK033 Is the sequence's behaviour under concurrent creation specified? Two reports created
      for one project at the same instant must not contend for the same number, and nothing states
      whether that is achieved by uniqueness refusal and retry, by a lock, or otherwise.
      [Gap, Spec §FR-002]
- [ ] CHK034 Is a gap in the sequence permitted, and is it significant? A numbered site register
      with a missing number is the first thing an auditor asks about, and the requirement is silent.
      [Gap, Spec §FR-002, Research §2]
- [ ] CHK035 Is the uniqueness FR-002 relies on stated as a requirement, or inherited from a
      database constraint written in August for a different purpose? [Traceability, Spec §FR-002]
- [ ] CHK036 Is the format specified closely enough to be stable? "Derives from the project" permits
      several renderings, and a report number appears on paper that people file.
      [Clarity, Spec §FR-002]

## Findings

Three items above are not open questions for a reviewer but defects in the written requirements,
found while generating this checklist. Each needs a decision before implementation, and each is
left unchecked for that reason.

**CHK003 — FR-003 and FR-030b contradict each other.** FR-003 says the system must compute
"**every** measurement line's quantity itself, as the product of the six factors". FR-030b says the
six factors must not be read for a presence-paid line. Both cannot hold. The intended reading is
clearly that FR-003 governs measured lines only, and FR-030a already says the quantity in force
depends on the payment basis — but FR-003 is the earlier and more absolute statement, and an
implementer following the requirements in order would meet it first. The fix is one word in FR-003.

**CHK016 — FR-039 obliges a report and specifies no response to it.** It requires the discrepancy
between the stored counter and the aggregate to be reportable, and says nothing about who is told,
whether either figure may be repaired, or which is authoritative when they differ. This is the
requirement most at risk of being satisfied and useless: an endpoint that returns a number nobody
is obliged to act on. Research §6 argues convincingly for *why* the check must exist; nothing states
what it is for.

**CHK024 — FR-040 does not say that a skipped isolation test must report as skipped.** The existing
probe suites warn to the console and return when the role cannot `CREATE ROLE`, which a continuous
integration summary renders indistinguishably from a pass. The requirement obliges the coverage and
not its visibility — and this feature's whole reason for carrying FR-040 is that an isolation policy
which was never in force looked, for two months, exactly like one that was.

A fourth, smaller: **CHK026/CHK027 — FR-040's scope is self-satisfying as written.** This feature
creates no table, so "every table this feature writes MUST carry the tenant isolation policy" is
already true and obliges nothing; and `BOQTaskItem`, which this feature does write, is arguably
pulled in by the same clause. The obligation that matters — prove the two existing policies under a
role that cannot bypass them — is the one the sentence states least clearly.
